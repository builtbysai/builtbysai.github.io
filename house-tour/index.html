Know before you offer

Converted garage. Whether the conversion was permitted, and whether the 1,608 sq ft includes the converted space, is not stated anywhere in the listing. Ask about permits, heat in that room, and whether it counts in the living area.
Solar panels. Panels are visible on the rear roof in the agent's photos. Owned or leased, and whether they convey with the sale, is not stated. Ask the agent.
Taxes disagree by source. The MLS lists $6,178.46 for 2026. Public records show $5,895 paid in 2026 (2025: $5,476, 2024: $5,372). The $283 gap is unresolved. Confirm from the tax bill before offer math.
Listing date quirk. The home entered the MLS November 13 as Coming Soon with an on-market date of December 1, then went active November 20. Treat December 1 as a stale date, not a future launch.

Flood zone is FEMA Zone X, minimal risk, Flood Factor 1 out of 10. No flood insurance requirement is indicated by Zone X, but confirm with your insurer and lender. The 2013 to 2014 sale was bank owned. No flood, lead, or other disclosure text appears in the listing feeds checked, and the full seller's disclosure was not available online. Public records differ slightly on the year built (1991 in the MLS, 1990 in PropertyShark) and the lot (0.11 acre in the MLS, 0.115 in PropertyShark).

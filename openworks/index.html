Proof, before I’m hired

One free sample audit, done already. This is what a Cherry Hill business gets on day one.

Michaelangelo’s Italian Restaurant

1334 Brace Rd, Cherry Hill, NJ · Sample audit, November 2026

Family-owned since 1989. About 4.4 stars across roughly 480–500 Google reviews, and regulars who praise it by name. A well-loved restaurant whose online presence is split across two websites, two phone numbers, and conflicting hours.

  • F1Two phone numbers. The brand site lists (856) 428-3231. The ordering site’s footer lists (856) 408-1574. Directory listings repeat both.
  • F2Conflicting hours. The ordering site says 11am–8pm and omits Thursday entirely. Customers checking hours get the wrong answer.
  • F3One menu, two prices. Fettuccine Alfredo is $23.95 in one section and $24.95 in another. The same for mozzarella sticks and eggplant parm.

Five fixes, all small, all built on information the restaurant already has:

  1. One listing sweep to a single set of facts, then automated monthly drift checks.
  2. An AI assistant for the questions the phone already answers: reservations, catering, and “is this the Cherry Hill location?”
  3. Review-response drafts in the owner’s voice, approved before anything posts.
  4. The weekly specials and the 200-item menu, turned into varied social posts — and an Instagram presence, which the restaurant lacks entirely.
  5. An automated menu-consistency check that flags every price mismatch and missing photo.
